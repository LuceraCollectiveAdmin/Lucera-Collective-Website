Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Lucera Collective. If you have any questions about how we handle personal data, contact us at zebastian@luceracollective.com.

What information we collect

This website has no user accounts, contact forms, or newsletter sign-up. We only receive personal information if you choose to contact us, for example by email. In that case we receive your name, email address, and whatever you write in your message.

How we use it

We use the information you send us only to reply to you and to discuss possible projects with you. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any project that follows, and then delete them.

Cookies and third-party services

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The website uses some external services that may process technical data such as your IP address when the page loads:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website
  • Google Fonts, which provides the fonts used on the site
  • Spotify, whose embedded players on our Artists page may set their own cookies

If you use the Spotify players or follow our links to Spotify or Instagram, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Just send us an email. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
